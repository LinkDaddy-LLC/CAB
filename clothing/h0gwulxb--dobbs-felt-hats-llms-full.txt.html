How to Care for Your Dobbs Felt Hat To preserve the longevity and pristine condition of your Dobbs felt hat, understanding how to care for it is of the utmost importance. This piece of headwear is an investment that deserves proper attention. Start by brushing your hat gently with a hat brush to remove dust and debris, being mindful of the directional fibers. For more intensive cleaning, a damp cloth can help to tackle tougher stains, but always ensure that the hat is air-dried away from direct sunlight to prevent warping or fading. For further protection, consider using a spray-on protective treatment designed for felt hats, which can shield your accessory from water damage and stains. When not in use, store your hat upright in a cool, dry area to maintain its shape, and avoid placing heavy items on top of it.
